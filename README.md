# Lexica

**A fast, falling-tile word game.** Letters drop into the draw pool. Grab them, build words on the board, and race the clock for the highest score.

Free. No ads. No accounts. No data collected. Lexica never connects to the internet.

## How to play

- **Catch letters.** Tiles fall into the draw pool. Touch or click a tile to freeze it, then drag it onto the board. Tiles you don't want can be dragged back to the pool, and they keep falling.
- **Build words.** The board has 5 rows of 8 cells. A tile locks to the row it was first placed in. You can reorder tiles within a row by dragging one onto another to swap them. Drop a tile on the discard area to send it back to the pool, where it can later be placed in any row.
- **Submit a row.** A word is a run of 2 or more neighboring tiles. If the word is in the dictionary it scores and the row clears. Longer words are worth more, thanks to a word-length bonus that starts at 4 letters (10) and rises through 5, 6 and 7 letters (20, 30, 40) to 8 letters (50).
- **Pick a theme.** Before you play, choose a theme: Animals, Fashion, Food, Games, Music or Tech. Words from the current theme's bonus list score **5x**, so spelling *seahorse* in an Animals game is worth far more than an ordinary word.
- **Beat the clock.** A standard game is two 5-minute rounds followed by a 2-minute **Bonus Round**, where letters come from a fixed pool of 60 tiles. Your score carries through all three rounds.

### Game modes

| Mode | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Standard** | Round 1, Round 2, then the Bonus Round. Your total goes on the high-score table. |
| **Endless** | Rounds repeat one after another. Each round is scored and recorded on its own. |
| **Anti-Lexica** | No falling tiles. All 40 cells start filled with jumbled letters, and two rows each hide a theme word. Find and arrange the words, then press the single checkmark to score every row at once. Wrong rows are crossed out in red so you can see your mistakes. |

### Settings

Tune the game on the Menu screen:

- **Tile Speed:** Slow, Medium or Fast. This controls only how quickly tiles fall.
- **Tile Number:** 2, 3 or 4 tiles in the pool at once.
- **Tile Pattern:** Steady, Burst or Chop. This changes the rhythm of the spawns without changing how many tiles arrive overall.
- **Difficulty:** Changes the mix of letters you are dealt.
- **Theme:** Animals, Fashion, Food, Games, Music or Tech.

Enter three-letter initials once per session, and your best scores are kept on the on-device high-score table.

## Words

Lexica checks words against the public-domain **ENABLE** word list (about 172,000 words), with a few changes:

- A short list of modern words that ENABLE lacks, such as *podcast*, *selfie* and *emoji*, has been added.
- Slurs, profanity and other sensitive words have been removed.
- Each theme's bonus words also always score during a game, even when they are not in the main list.

Only exact whole-word matches count. There is no spelling correction.

## Privacy

Lexica collects, shares and sells nothing.

- No accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking software.
- No internet permission. The app cannot send data anywhere.
- No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
- High scores and initials are stored only on your device and are deleted when you uninstall.
