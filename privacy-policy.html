Lexica Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Lexica ("the app") is a word game published by Syntheta.ai LLC. This policy explains what the app does with your information.
In short: Lexica does not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

What the app collects

Nothing is sent from your device. The app has no user accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking or data-collection software. It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files, and it does not have permission to connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

To work as a game, the app saves a small amount of information locally on your device, such as your high scores and the three-letter initials you choose to go with them. This information is stored in the app's own storage area, is never transmitted anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Google Play

If you installed Lexica from Google Play, Google may collect certain information about installs, crashes, and device types under its own privacy policy. That information is provided to the developer only in aggregate and cannot identify you. The app itself does not send anything to Google or anyone else.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Lexica collects any information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the Google Play listing.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@syntheta.ai.
